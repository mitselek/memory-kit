# dormant

Facts parked, not deleted: out of play, may return. Exempt from the live sweep, revisited on
the cadence each fact's `reviewed` field states. Parking moves one fact, not a subject file.

Empty by design. A truly dead fact is deleted -- git is the archive. A fact something live
still cites is not dormant.

## Caps at the moment of writing (source: caps.yaml)

- Facts per file: at most 30 (`dormant.per_file`).
